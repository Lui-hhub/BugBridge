## Preserved sources and generated representations

Raw template files are preserved byte-for-byte. Files under `normalized/` are
generated representations of the upstream forms, with BugBridge field-purpose
annotations and source provenance; the catalog adds import status and cache notes.
These generated representations include upstream template content and retain its
license and attribution. They are not a relicense of that content under MIT.

The catalog records raw/normalized hashes and revision provenance. Normal tasks
reuse cached templates without refetching them. If additional third-party template
snapshots are contributed, preserve their source revision, license and notices.
